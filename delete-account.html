Account and Data Deletion

At Mirra AI, we give users full control over their data.

To delete your account and all associated data:

  1. Open the Mirra AI app.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the page.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm your request.

Once confirmed, your account and associated data, including your wardrobe, preferences, and activity history, will be permanently deleted.

Please note that account deletion is irreversible. Once your account has been deleted, it cannot be recovered, and all associated data will be permanently removed from our systems.
